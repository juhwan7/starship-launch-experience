# Research baseline — 2026-09

## Confirmed official facts used in the experience

Primary source: SpaceX Starship vehicle page
- https://new.spacex.com/vehicles/starship
- Current public vehicle: 124 m total height, 9 m diameter.
- Starship: 52 m, 6 engines (3 sea-level Raptor + 3 Raptor Vacuum).
- Super Heavy: 72 m, 33 Raptors, with 13 maneuverable engines in the center and 20 around the perimeter.
- Propellants: sub-cooled liquid methane and liquid oxygen.

Primary source: SpaceX — Starship's Thirteenth Flight Test, 2026-07-24
- https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-13
- Second V3 flight.
- Super Heavy ignited all 33 Raptor 3 engines.
- Starship ignited six Raptors during hot-staging.
- Countdown reference: Ship LOX T−37:30, Booster LOX T−37:00, Booster methane T−35:25, Ship methane T−34:48, engine chill T−21:30, launch GO T−00:30, flame diverter T−00:17, booster engine start command T−00:03.
- Flight timeline reference: Max Q T+00:58, Super Heavy MECO T+02:18, hot-staging T+02:21.

Primary source: SpaceX — Starship's Twelfth Flight Test, 2026-05-22
- https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-12
- First V3 flight, first Raptor 3 flight, first Starship flight from Pad 2.

Primary source: SpaceX Updates — Introducing Starship V3
- https://new.spacex.com/updates
- V3 Super Heavy uses three larger grid fins rather than four.
- Integrated hot-stage replaces the previous single-use protective interstage.

Regulatory context: FAA Starship/Super Heavy Boca Chica pages
- https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship
- https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship/license_review_process

## Explicit approximations

The following are visual reconstructions, not claims of exact flight telemetry or manufacturing geometry:
- Tower/member spacing, tank-farm dimensions and pipe routing.
- Internal tank/bulkhead colors and cutaway fluid visualization.
- Camera path and time compression.
- Exhaust plume, vapor, dust and heat appearance.
- Browser-space ascent distance and Earth scale.
- Interpolated altitude/velocity shown between narrative stages.
- Post-separation relative motion.
